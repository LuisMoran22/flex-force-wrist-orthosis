# Methodology

The project was developed using a staged biomedical device design approach.

## 1. Research and Clinical Context

The team reviewed wrist fracture mechanisms, common sequelae, and limitations in rehabilitation monitoring. The problem was framed around reduced mobility, incomplete range of motion, and loss of strength after wrist injury.

## 2. Conceptual Design

The concept was defined as a wrist orthosis that could provide support while preserving useful mobility. The prototype was intended to be accessible, comfortable, and customizable.

## 3. Hardware Selection

The proposed hardware included:

- Arduino Nano
- Sensor Flex 25K
- MPU6050 IMU
- OLED 128x64 display
- EMG circuit
- Neoprene fabric
- ABS 3D-printed parts
- Copper board for PCB fabrication

## 4. Mechanical Design

SolidWorks was used to model the orthosis/hand support concept and visualize assembly and exploded-view configurations.

## 5. Electronics and EMG Stage

The EMG design concept included:

- AD620 instrumentation amplifier
- High-pass filtering
- Low-pass filtering
- Offset stage
- Mean absolute value processing concept
- Non-inverting amplification

## 6. Calibration

Calibration plots were generated for the sensor system and gyroscope. Future work should include structured calibration datasets, measurement uncertainty, and comparison with reference instruments.
