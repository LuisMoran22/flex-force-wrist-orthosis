# Project Summary

**Flex-Force** is a wrist orthosis prototype developed as an academic biomedical engineering project. The device concept was designed for patients with post-fracture wrist sequelae, particularly limitations in flexion, extension, strength, and functional mobility.

The proposed solution combines mechanical support with quantitative rehabilitation monitoring. The system concept integrates flexible sensors, an MPU6050 inertial measurement unit, an OLED display, and an EMG acquisition circuit for forearm muscle activity evaluation.

## Problem

Patients recovering from wrist fractures may lack objective data to evaluate their recovery progress. Many assessments depend on subjective perception or comparison with the unaffected hand.

## Proposed Solution

A customizable wrist orthosis capable of supporting the wrist while collecting rehabilitation-related measurements such as movement angles, force-related data, and EMG activity.

## Technical Areas

- Biomedical instrumentation
- Orthosis design
- Rehabilitation engineering
- Sensor integration
- EMG acquisition circuit design
- 3D modeling and prototyping
