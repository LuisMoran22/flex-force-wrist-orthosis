# MEXT / Interview Notes

## Short Explanation

Flex-Force was an academic biomedical engineering project focused on designing a smart wrist orthosis for rehabilitation monitoring after wrist fractures. The prototype combined mechanical support with sensor-based measurement, including flexible sensors, an MPU6050 inertial sensor, an OLED display, and an EMG circuit concept.

## Strong Interview Answer

This project helped me understand how biomedical engineering can connect mechanical design, electronics, sensors, and rehabilitation needs. The objective was not only to build a supportive wrist orthosis, but also to obtain quantitative information that could help evaluate patient recovery more objectively.

## Skills to Emphasize

- Biomedical instrumentation
- Rehabilitation engineering
- Arduino-based prototyping
- EMG signal acquisition concepts
- 3D modeling in SolidWorks
- Hardware integration
- Clinical problem framing

## Connection With Graduate Research

This project is related to my interest in AI and biomedical systems because it shows how sensor data can be collected from a healthcare device. In future research, similar sensor-based systems could be combined with data analysis or machine learning to monitor rehabilitation progress and support clinical decision-making.
