# EMG Circuit Notes

The project included an EMG circuit concept for forearm muscle activity acquisition.

## Main Stages

- **Instrumentation amplifier:** AD620-based stage.
- **Low-pass filter:** Proposed cutoff frequency around 1 kHz.
- **High-pass filter:** Signal conditioning stage.
- **Offset circuit:** Used to shift the signal for microcontroller-compatible reading.
- **Mean Absolute Value stage:** Concept for EMG envelope or rectified signal processing.
- **Non-inverting amplifier:** Additional gain stage.

## Notes

Future work should validate the EMG circuit with safety considerations, proper electrode placement, isolation, noise reduction, and comparison with reference EMG equipment.
